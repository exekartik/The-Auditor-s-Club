The Auditors Club (TAC) primarily operates as a professional financial operations, corporate compliance, and advisory practice. This Return Policy clarifies how service cancellations, revisions, and physical item returns are handled.

1. Professional & Digital Service Scope

The majority of services provided by TAC (such as company incorporation filings, virtual CFO advisory, tax planning, financial modeling, and investor pitch deck preparation) are intangible, digital professional services.

  • Intangible advisory services cannot be physically returned once rendered.
  • If you are dissatisfied with a digital deliverable (e.g., investor pitch deck or financial planning model), TAC provides up to 3 complimentary revision iterations to refine the work to your exact requirements.

2. Service Engagement Cancellation

Clients may request to return or cancel an unfulfilled service package before statutory filings or advisory work commences:

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to info@theauditorsclub.com along with your engagement reference number.
  • If work has partially commenced, a pro-rata adjustment will be calculated based on hours logged or milestone completed.

3. Physical Corporate Kits & Physical Items

For physical deliverables provided as part of company incorporation packages (such as official company seals, share certificates, or printed corporate folders):

  • Defective or Damaged Dispatches: If a physical corporate item arrives damaged or incorrectly engraved, TAC will replace and reship the item free of charge within 7 business days of notice.
  • Return Shipping: Notice of damaged physical goods must be reported within 48 hours of courier delivery with photo evidence emailed to support.

4. Document Revision Guarantee

To ensure 100% client satisfaction on advisory projects (e.g., investor readiness assessments, due diligence data rooms, or valuation reports), TAC assigns a dedicated advisory partner to review and update deliverables until key project objectives are fulfilled.
